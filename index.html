Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Open the app and sign up with your email or Google account, then choose whether you are a teacher or an institution.

How do I reset my password?
On the login screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the instructions sent to your email.

How do I delete my account?
Email us at spcolonna@gmail.com from your registered address and we'll process your request.
